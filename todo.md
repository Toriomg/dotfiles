High value, low effort:
  - cliphist + wl-clipboard — clipboard history for Wayland. Bind SUPER+V to open
  history in wofi. This is the single most-missed QoL feature on a fresh rice.
  - swayosd — on-screen overlay for volume/brightness keys. Right now pressing
  XF86AudioRaiseVolume gives no visual feedback at all. SwayOSD shows a clean pill
  indicator.
  - wlsunset — night light / blue light filter that adjusts color temperature by
  time of day. Set it, forget it.

  Medium effort, meaningful:
  - Window rules — auto-float and center common popups (pavucontrol,
  blueman-manager, nm-connection-editor). Right now they tile like normal windows.
  - hyprpolkitagent — authentication dialogs for sudo GUI actions. Without it, some
  apps silently fail when they need elevated permissions.
  - wf-recorder — screen recording. You have screenshots covered, recording is the
  gap. Bind a key, output to ~/Videos/.

  Nice to have:
  - Emoji picker — rofimoji with a wofi backend, bind to SUPER+.
  - Per-workspace app rules — auto-send browser to workspace 2, Discord to 5, etc.
  on launch.
